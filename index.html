GogoFakta Publisher is a private publishing tool used by the creator of the GogoFakta channel to upload its own short Indonesian fun-fact and trivia videos to the creator's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The creator produces short vertical videos (fun facts, trivia, short stories) on their own server.
  2. The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) once and authorizes the app.
  3. Each finished video is sent to TikTok through the Content Posting API with a caption written by the creator. The creator reviews each post and can cancel it.

The app is not offered to the public. It only publishes content created by and owned by the account owner. It does not read, collect or sell data from other TikTok users.
