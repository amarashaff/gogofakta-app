Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains how GogoFakta Publisher (the "App") handles data when you connect a TikTok account.

1. Data we receive

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope, used only to show which account is connected.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, used only to upload videos you choose to publish.
  • Publishing status of videos the App uploaded (for example: processing, published, failed).

2. How we use data

Data is used only to authenticate the account owner and to upload and publish the owner's own videos. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok as required by the API.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted-at-rest on a private server accessible only to the owner and are never published. Uploaded video files are deleted from the server after a retention period.

5. Data of other users

The App does not collect, read or store data about other TikTok users, followers or viewers.

6. Your choices

You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. After revocation, stored tokens are deleted. You may request deletion of any stored data via the contact below.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
